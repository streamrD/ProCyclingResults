Five marks for Pro Cycling Results, all drawn from the site's existing UCI palette. A favicon lives at 16 pixels most of its life, so each is shown at working size on both a light and a dark tab bar — that, not the big version, is where these are won or lost.

Picking one

Tell me the name and I'll add it to the site: it ships as a single assets/favicon.svg plus a <link rel="icon"> in the document head, which is the whole change. Every mark is plain SVG, so it stays crisp at any size and costs about a kilobyte.

If you pick Mono, it needs a prefers-color-scheme rule inside the SVG so the blue frame doesn't disappear against a dark tab bar — the others carry their own background and are safe either way.
