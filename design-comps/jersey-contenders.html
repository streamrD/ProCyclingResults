What the sources actually give

  • Every stage race has the tables. All 15 stage races on the site carry a “Classification standings” section, in one of two wikitext forms — plain wikitables on the Grand Tours, {{cyclingresult}} blocks on the smaller races. Both already have parsers here.
  • Not all of them are points. Points and mountains are scored in points; general, young rider and team are times. The card prints the column its source gives rather than calling everything “points”.
  • The standings lag the jerseys by a stage. Wikipedia updates the leadership table before the standings tables, so the card is labelled with its own stage.
  • Some jerseys have no table. Tour de Pologne’s “Polish rider”, the Giro’s “Red Bull KM”: those rows stay plain, with no hover.
  • The team row lists teams, not riders. Same card, no flags-and-team second line.
