# Area 1: Data Pipeline Reliability

Assessed commit: `9dc326f` (confirmed live on production via `/api/build-info`, `source: railway-env`). Assessment date: 2026-09-26, the evening of the women's Worlds road race. Line numbers refer to `server.js` at that commit unless another file is named.

## 1. Summary

**Grade: C+ from the reader's point of view** (B for a live Grand Tour, D for one-day race days and for detection). The live stage-race path is genuinely good: official ASO/RCS providers put a stage result on the card about four minutes after the finish, a self-arming timer rebuilds every 60 s in host-country racing hours, providers run against a 2.5 s budget so a slow origin never blanks first paint, and the robustness harness shows every main parser returns empty rather than throwing or inventing data when fed garbage (112 empty, 0 throws on the parsers proper). Three things matter most. First, half the calendar is one-day races and they ride a different, much slower path: a one-day WorldTour race is in no bucket on its own race day (it needs a winner to be "recent" and a future start to be "upcoming"), and its winner arrives through the hourly metadata cache, so on 10 October Il Lombardia will be off the page from the start until up to ~75 minutes after Wikipedia's season table names the winner; the fix for exactly this was applied to the Worlds only (`isWorldChampionshipEventAwaitingResult`, line 7651: "rather than vanishing for the afternoon"). Second, nothing detects a silent-empty or thin card: there is one `console.log` in 17,112 lines, 41 `catch` blocks swallow upstream errors, `/api/data-status` and `/api/build-info` carry no source health, and every one of the ~30 incidents recorded this season was found by a reader or by an agent in a session, none by a test or a monitor; production today has a finished Grand-Tour-tier race (La Vuelta Femenina) showing a three-name GC and ten of fourteen finished one-day cards showing three names instead of five, and nobody was told. Third, two parsers are wrong-but-plausible or site-emptying under realistic drift with no detection: the Cyclingnews nationals parser is positional and never reads its header row (a column insertion or reorder mislabels every champion in 105 federations), and the season-table parser is anchored to an exact table class and column order (five of seven realistic variants empty the whole site, and the 2027 rollover depends on it against pages that do not exist yet).

## 6. Strengths

- **The live Grand Tour path is measured, not guessed.** Freshness was timed against the source on 2026-09-05 (finish 15:48, lavuelta.es 15:50-15:52, production 15:51:42), and the two changes it produced (self-arming timer at 8407, trailing GC kept one stage behind at 5224) are each tested ("a live race rebuilds itself on a timer…", "mergeStageRaceSnapshots keeps a general classification one stage behind…").
- **Bounded blocking, with the right sentinel.** `OFFICIAL_SNAPSHOT_BLOCKING_BUDGET_MS` (6900) with `OFFICIAL_SNAPSHOT_TIMED_OUT` distinct from `null`, late results merged through the same `selectPreferredStageRaceSnapshot` and re-settled by `applyCanonicalRiderNames` (6939-6958). The rejected alternative (skipping providers for finished races) was measured and its damage recorded, so the next person will not repeat it.
- **Considerate fetching that is written down and counted.** Revision-index change detection (one query per 50 titles, `maxlag`), three-connection concurrency, host-country racing hours, 6 h settled caches, a YouTube UA exception with its reason, and a public `DATA-SOURCES.md` whose review log matches the code (every TTL in the table checked against the constants at 24-125). Request counts came from a counting harness, and the doc says so.
- **Parsers fail empty, not loud and not wrong.** Across 236 harness runs the wikitext and HTML parsers never threw and never fabricated a rider; renamed classification headers on the main article path made no difference at all (the `{{cyclingresult}}`/wikitable readers key on structure, not header text).
- **Honest fallbacks where the product chose them.** The generic stage pictogram, the nationals "temporarily unavailable" banner, the "Overall after stage N" label on a trailing GC, the "Today" Worlds card and the medal-summary bridge (which worked as designed this evening: `resultSource: wikipedia-medal-summary`, podium, video and eight stories on the women's road race card).
- **Every incident left a fixture and a note.** 198 tests; ASO, RCS, Greece, YouTube, Worlds (four layouts) and Vuelta shapes captured from real pages; a handoff that names the trap, the date and why the obvious fix was wrong. The "run it against every earlier edition first" rule found 24 pages' worth of Worlds layouts before race week.
- **Production-first debugging is institutional.** Four sessions running, the first step was to curl the deployed payload, and `stageRace.provenance` exists to make that useful.
